- [from sky r3] `ctx.state.daylight`'s curve changed: the knee moved from sun-elevation 0.38 to 0.26, so dawn and dusk now score much higher (05:53 was 0.26, is now 0.47; 06:12 is 0.69). That is deliberate — the old curve is why 05:53 rendered as flat night across the whole game — but if it breaks a timed gag, key off `sky.phase` ('dawn'|'day'|'dusk'|'night') or `sky.lampMix` instead of a daylight number.
